03 / UNDER THE SHEET

No medium.
No mystery API.

Real, open-weight AI. MiniLM turns each passage into a 384-dimensional vector. Cosine similarity retrieves the closest present-day echo, even when the words have changed.

Every finding has a source. Inspect the old line, its nearest current passage, alternative matches, and a word-overlap baseline. Nothing is generated or attributed to a person.

Human judgment stays visible. Keep, retire, or investigate a promise with a note. Export the source fingerprints, scores, model metadata, and your decision.

Limits, privacy & model credits

Built for English short prose. Headings and fenced code are excluded; long passages and oversized documents are rejected. The model has a 256-token limit. Similarity cannot establish whether a feature exists, whether a claim is true, or whether two sentences agree. Negation warnings are a vocabulary heuristic and can miss contradictions.

The app loads its model and runtime from the same local server. No analytics, remote model calls, or persistent storage. Text and review notes are cleared on reload. Downloaded reports contain excerpts of your input; share them deliberately.
